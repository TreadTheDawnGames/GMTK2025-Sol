<h1>SOL</h1>
<p>You are the cartographer of the newly discovered SOL system. Scan planets by orbiting them and collect 2500 points of data in one combo or collect all of the artifacts to complete your mission!</p>
<p>SOL is an arcade physics game about looping planets to gain as many points as you can.</p>
<b><p>This project was originally created in 96 hours by three people&nbsp;for GMTK Game Jam 2025.</p></b>
<h4>Points:</h4>
<ul><li>The first time you orbit a planet, +5 orbit points instead of 1.</li><li>Entering a planet's atmosphere adds +1 to your multiplier. Bouncing a planet doubles it.</li><li>The number of points you score in a combo is the number of orbits you did times the number of unique planets you visited.</li></ul>
